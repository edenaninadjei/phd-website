##  About me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Government at the University of Essex and a current recipient of the Political Studies Association Diverse Voices Student Programme Award. My research focuses on the political identities of British African communities, with a particular focus on British Ghanaians and Nigerians. I’m interested in how cultural norms, religious beliefs, and the political cultures of countries of origin shape identity and voting behaviour across generations in the diaspora.

Beyond my doctoral research, I serve as a Race Equity Research Fellow at the University of Surrey, where I lead key initiatives within the Surrey Black Scholars programme. Funded by Research England (RE) and the Office for Students (OfS), this project is committed to widening participation and empowering Black British postgraduate researchers
